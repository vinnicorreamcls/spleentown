    Frequently Asked Questions AboutSpleen Town Noir Cocktail Bar

    Where is Spleen Town located in San Francisco and which neighborhoods are nearby?

    Spleen Town is on Leavenworth St in Nob Hill, near Tenderloin, Lower Nob Hill, Union Square, Russian Hill and hotels in the surrounding area.

    Do I need a reservation, or do you accept walk-ins?

    For reservations and walk-in questions, contact Spleen Town via WhatsApp at +1 (415) 673-1211.

    Can I host a private event or rent out the bar for a group in San Francisco?

    Yes. Private cocktail events, tailored celebrations and private lounge rentals can be discussed via WhatsApp at +1 (415) 673-1211.

    What are Spleen Town's opening hours and till what time is the bar open?

    Spleen Town is open daily from 12:00 PM until 02:00 AM.
